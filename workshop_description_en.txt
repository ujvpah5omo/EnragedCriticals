[h1]Enraged Criticals[/h1]

[b]This is a server-side mod. Players do not need to install it on their clients.[/b]

This mod adds damage overload, Enrage stacking, and critical hit mechanics to bosses with the [b]epic[/b] tag, as long as they have both [b]health[/b] and [b]combat[/b] components.

When a boss takes too much damage from players within a short time window, it becomes enraged. While enraged, the boss gains damage reduction, temporarily ignores sleep, freeze, grogginess, and similar control effects, and has a chance to deal critical hits. Critical hits add armor-piercing true damage and cause extra armor durability loss.

[h2]Core Mechanics[/h2]

[list]
[*]Damage tracking window: 4 seconds
[*]Trigger threshold: clamp(Boss max health * 6%, 500, 1800)
[*]Enrage duration: 10 seconds
[*]Control immunity after trigger or refresh: 3 seconds
[*]Critical chance: 50%
[*]Maximum Enrage stacks: 4
[/list]

When a boss takes damage from a player or a player follower, the mod records recent damage dealt within the last 4 seconds. Once the accumulated damage reaches the threshold, the boss becomes enraged.

[list]
[*]First trigger: Enrage Stack 1
[*]Triggering again while enraged: Enrage stacks increase by 1, up to 4 stacks
[*]Each trigger or refresh clears the accumulated damage record
[*]Each trigger or refresh grants 3 seconds of control immunity
[*]Enrage duration refreshes to 10 seconds
[*]When Enrage ends naturally, stacks reset to 0
[/list]

[h2]Enrage Effects[/h2]

[list]
[*]Removes sleep, freeze, grogginess, and similar control effects
[*]Continues clearing these control effects for 3 seconds
[*]The boss turns red, with higher stacks making it redder
[*]The boss gains damage reduction
[*]While enraged, the boss has a 50% chance to critically hit players
[*]Critical hits add armor-piercing true damage
[*]Critical hits also cause extra armor durability loss
[/list]

[h2]Stack Values[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Stack 1[/b]: Boss takes 75% damage, critical true damage 15%, extra armor loss 30%
[*][b]Stack 2[/b]: Boss takes 65% damage, critical true damage 25%, extra armor loss 45%
[*][b]Stack 3[/b]: Boss takes 55% damage, critical true damage 35%, extra armor loss 60%
[*][b]Stack 4[/b]: Boss takes 50% damage, critical true damage 45%, extra armor loss 75%
[/list]

[h2]Critical Damage[/h2]

Critical true damage uses the highest value among:

[list]
[*]The boss's default attack damage
[*]original_damage
[*]damage
[/list]

This means bosses like Deerclops will not have their critical true damage reduced just because the base game halves their damage against players.

[h2]Default Threshold Examples[/h2]

The default threshold is not simply 6%. It is:

[code]clamp(Boss max health * 6%, 500, 1800)[/code]

[list]
[*]Deerclops: 4000 health, 6% = 240, actual trigger threshold = 500
[*]Moose/Goose: 6000 health, 6% = 360, actual trigger threshold = 500
[*]Bearger: 6000 health, 6% = 360, actual trigger threshold = 500
[*]Dragonfly: 27500 health, 6% = 1650, actual trigger threshold = 1650
[/list]

[h2]Gameplay Feel[/h2]

For lower-health bosses such as Deerclops, Moose/Goose, and Bearger, [b]500 damage[/b] is the key breakpoint.

[list]
[*]A normal character with a Dark Sword or Glass Cutter can reliably trigger Enrage solo
[*]A Ham Bat can trigger it solo only with very high attack uptime; 2 players are more reliable
[*]Spear, Battle Spear, and Tentacle Spike usually require 2 players
[/list]

For high-health bosses like Dragonfly, stable Enrage triggers and repeated stacking usually require coordinated high-damage multiplayer focus fire.

[list]
[*]3 normal characters with Dark Swords or Glass Cutters can trigger it at the limit; 4 players are more reliable
[*]4 players with Ham Bats are reliable
[*]Lower-damage weapons usually require around 5 to 7 players
[/list]

For max-mightiness Wolfgang, damage is doubled, so Enrage becomes much easier to trigger.

[list]
[*]Deerclops, Moose/Goose, and Bearger: Wolfgang can trigger Enrage solo even with a Spear
[*]Dragonfly: 2 max-mightiness Wolfgangs with Ham Bats, Dark Swords, or Glass Cutters can trigger Enrage fairly reliably
[/list]

[h2]Design Goal[/h2]

This mechanic makes high-damage weapons, Wolfgang, and multiplayer focus fire more likely to trigger boss Enrage, creating higher risk for high burst damage strategies. Lower-damage kiting playstyles are affected less.

In short:

[list]
[*]Lower damage: safer, but the fight takes longer
[*]High burst damage: faster, but more likely to enrage and stack the boss
[*]Multiplayer focus fire: more efficient, but also more dangerous
[/list]
